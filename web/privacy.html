Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Campanile does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings (language, reminder times, reader size and theme), the prayers you opened today and the texts the app has downloaded are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally with macOS notifications. Nothing about them leaves your Mac.

Downloads

To show the traditional Roman Office, Campanile downloads one file per day from f-n-ll.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests carry no personal information, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to GitHub, which handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If a future version shows official liturgical texts from another source, this page will say which one before that version is released.

Purchases

Campanile Plus is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Plus is active on your Mac. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Campanile is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
