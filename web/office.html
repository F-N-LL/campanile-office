Campanile Office

Daily texts of the traditional Roman Office (Rubrics 1960), in English and Latin, for the Campanile Mac app. Files: v1/<en|la>/<YYYY-MM-DD>.json, listed in v1/index.json.

Rendered with the engine and data of the Divinum Officium Project (MIT License). The liturgical texts are in the public domain: the Breviarium Romanum, the Vulgate, the Douay-Rheims Bible, the Marquess of Bute's Roman Breviary (1908), Britt's Hymns of the Breviary and Missal (1922) and Stanbrook Abbey's Day Hours of the Church (1921).
